# [DEVCON 2: Turf Panic ] 

## Objective

> Our objective with this game was to try and make a territory claiming game that was 
> intuitive and easy to pick up.
>
> Did we accomplish this objective? 
> Measurably, Yes. 

## Game Mechanic

> The game mechanic we aimed to make is a simple territorial control painting based game. 
> Players would move across a small one on one single screen arena attempting to cover
> as much ground as possible in their colour. 


## Design Rationale

> Our design as it relates to the broader objective we set out is in trying to 
> find new and innovative ways of applying the territory claiming formula of a game like 
> **"Splatoon"**. The rationale was that by simplifying the gameplay loop we could 
> attempt to discover new applications from first principles. 


## Cutting Room Floor 

> In addition to what we decided to develop, there was a significant amount of alternative 
> design that was toyed with in discussion. Things like a 60 second exploration game where
> the map is significantly larger than can be explored within the timeframe, giving a lot 
> of replayability in a tight yet well constructed package. This exploration game 
> was suggested primarily because of our teams strengths in level design, however given
> the time constraints of development it wasn't as strongly considered. 
>
> There were also things like a tank vs tank game similar to the "Wii Play" minigame 
> "Tanks!"  
> A racing game was briefly considered on the same basis as the tank game, but both 
> concepts were quickly shuttered.
> In the end, Diogo Suggested something akin to 2D Splatoon, and it was agreed upon.
