### Design & Gameplay
When making a game for an arcade machine, it's important to ensure that the controls of said game are simple, and easy to remember as arcade cabinets typically have only a few buttons, and a joystick. With that in mind we set out to make something which provided people with enjoyment, while staying within those restraints.<br><br>
We ended up landing on a simple racing game, with inspiration from the story of John Henry our group designed a “Man Vs. Machine” type aesthetic. The game involves two players racing on a railway, one takes control of a raccoon and mouse who are speeding down the rails on a handcart, while the second player controls a steam train barrelling down the tracks.<br><br>
For controls, player one uses the arcade cabinet’s two face buttons, interchanging which button they press to move their cart forward. Meanwhile player two will shift the joystick back and forth to progress the train on the tracks. These controls were designed with the idea of pumping the handcart’s lever back and forth, and shovelling coal into the furnace of the train respectively.

### Objective
Our objective statement with this project was to express the competition between man and machine, while our gameplay goal being to have two different but equal control schemes to heighten that difference, while also delivering a fun, yet competitive experience.<br><br>
Thinking back to classic arcade games, many of them were two players. This gave people the chance to have an enjoyable and fun experience with their friends, commonly these two player games were competitive in nature. This was the feeling we aimed to capture with our mini game. We wanted to create something which someone could easily start with with a friend of theirs, understand the goal, and the controls easily and have a fun time racing against their friend for a few rounds.
